How to Play

Guess the secret Eternal Return character using color-coded clues

Making a Guess

Click the input box — a full character list appears. Type to filter, then click a name or press Enter to submit your guess. Each guess fills a row in the table with color hints.

Color Meanings

Green — Exact match. This attribute is correct.
Orange — Partial match. At least one weapon or damage type overlaps, or the release year/season is close but not exact.
Black — No match. This attribute is completely wrong.

Column Guide

Weapons
Green if all weapons match exactly. Orange if at least one weapon is shared with the secret character.
Gender
Green (correct) or Black (wrong). No partial match.
Range
Melee, Ranged, or Mixed. Green or Black only.
Damage Type
Green if exact match. Orange if at least one damage type overlaps.
Difficulty
Rated 1–5. Green or Black only.
Release Year
Green = exact year.
↑ Orange = secret is 1 year later.
↑↑ Orange = secret is 2+ years later.
↓ Black = secret is 1 year earlier.
↓↓ Black = secret is 2+ years earlier.
Release Season
Same arrow system as Release Year but tracks season order. Early Access seasons come before Official seasons.

Difficulty Modes

🗓 Daily Challenge

Same for all
Everyone gets the same secret character each day. Resets at midnight.
10 guesses
Maximum 10 guesses per day. Make them count.
Streaks
Win days in a row to build your streak 🔥. Miss a day or fail and your streak resets.

After finishing, copy your emoji result grid and share it with friends without spoiling the answer.
Yesterday
The previous day's character is shown at the top of the daily screen.

Tips

💡
Start with characters that cover many different weapon types, genders, and release years to narrow things down fast.
🔍
Already-guessed characters are greyed out in the dropdown so you never accidentally guess the same character twice.
⬛
In Nightmare mode, some cells show ? — those columns give no color hint at all. Use the columns that ARE visible to deduce the answer.
📅
In Daily mode, your progress is saved automatically. If you close the page mid-game, your guesses will still be there when you come back.
↑↑
Double arrows mean the gap is 2 or more years/seasons away. Single arrow means just 1 away — you're very close!
