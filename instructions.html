How to Play

Guess the secret Eternal Return character using color-coded clues

Making a Guess

Click the input box — a full character list appears. Type to filter by name, then click a character or press Enter to submit. Each guess fills a row in the table with color-coded feedback.

Color Meanings

Green — Exact match. This attribute is completely correct.
Orange — Partial match. At least one weapon or damage type overlaps with the secret character, or the release year/season is close but not exact.
Black — No match. This attribute is completely wrong.

Column Guide

Weapons
Green if all weapons match exactly. Orange if at least one weapon is shared with the secret character.
Gender
Green (correct) or Black (wrong). No partial match.
Range
Melee, Ranged, or Mixed. Green or Black only.
Damage Type
Green if exact match. Orange if at least one damage type overlaps.
Difficulty
Rated 1–5. Green (exact) or Black (wrong) only.
Release Year
Green = exact year.   ↑ Orange = secret is 1 year later.   ↑↑ Orange = 2+ years later.   ↓ Black = 1 year earlier.   ↓↓ Black = 2+ years earlier.
Release Season
Same arrow system as Release Year but by season order. Early Access seasons come before Official seasons in the timeline.

Difficulty Modes

ModeGuessesSpecial Rules
Easy Unlimited All columns visible. Take as long as you need.
Normal 10 All columns visible. Standard experience.
Hard 5 All columns visible. Think carefully before each guess.
Nightmare 5 2–3 random columns are hidden every game. Hidden cells show ? with no color hint. You won't know which columns are hidden until you start guessing.

🗓 Daily Challenge

Same for all
Everyone gets the same secret character each day. Resets at midnight.
10 guesses
Maximum 10 guesses per day — no difficulty selection in daily mode.
Streaks 🔥
Win consecutive days to build your streak. Miss a day or fail and your streak resets to zero.
Share result
After finishing, copy your emoji grid (🟩🟧⬛) and share with friends — no spoilers!
Auto-save
Progress is saved automatically. Close and reopen the page anytime — your guesses will still be there.
Yesterday
The previous day's character is revealed at the top of the daily screen each new day.

Tips

💡
Start with characters that cover many different weapon types, genders, and release years to narrow things down fast.
🔍
Already-guessed characters are greyed out in the dropdown — you can never accidentally repeat a guess.
❓
In Nightmare mode, cells showing ? give no hint at all. Focus on the visible columns to work out the answer.
↑↑
Double arrows mean 2+ years/seasons away. Single arrow means just 1 away — you're very close to the right year!
📅
In Daily mode your progress is saved automatically. Come back later and your guesses will still be there.
